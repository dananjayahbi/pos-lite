# PayHere Links

PayHere Links is an Innovative way to accept payments directly from your facebook page, website or even simply through an Email, Chat or an SMS sent to your customer via sharing a PayHere Payment Link. Specially if you run your business mainly through your facebook/instagram page, PayHere Links is the ideal solution for you.

You can not-only accept the payments, but also can accept the orders online with PayHere Links as PayHere collects your customer details, delivery address & preferred item details on behalf of you. That means it simply automates your order accepting process as well. You just have to add your items to PayHere Merchant Portal & process the automated orders you receive via PayHere Links.

PayHere Links can also be used to accept payments via Emails, Chat or SMS. If you send Invoices to your customers for your products or services via Emails, WhatsApp, Viber or SMS, you can include a PayHere Link also in that, for your customer to make the payment for your Invoice just by a click of a link.

##### Accepting payments with PayHere Links

It’s just 3 simple steps.

  1. Create a PayHere Link
  2. Copy it
  3. Place it on your facebook/instagram post or send through Email, Chat or SMS



These steps are quite straightforward & simple to follow. But to clarify you in detail, here are the specific detailed steps you need to follow to fulfill the above steps.

### 1\. Create a PayHere Link

  1. Sign in to your PayHere Account & go to 'Payment Links' section from the side menu.
  2. Click 'Create Link' button & fill the ‘Item name’, amount.
  3. Click ‘Create Link’ button & then you can see the PayHere Link you just created.   
  




### 2\. Copy it

  1. Find your Item name from the list of Payment Links 
  2. Click on Copy icon in front of the Payment Link   
  




### 3\. Place it or Send it

  1. Go to your item’s facebook post, web page or compose an Email, Chat or SMS to your customer
  2. Type some instruction on payment such as “Pay with PayHere: “
  3. Paste the copied PayHere Link in front of that instruction   
  




That’s it. Now your customers can simply click on this link & pay you easily.
